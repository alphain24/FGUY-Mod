Paint it yourself

Skin pack

Outfit pieces made in the Models tab (a hat, a vest...) can come with the skin.
Its picture in the game's menus (its skin tile, the HUD's head): a drawing of your own, on a transparent background - or, without one, the outfit's own picture (PICTURE in the textures), with your changes.

Make a mod

One file to share: this skin (if you've changed something) and any other packs you add. Players put it in the game's FGUYMod\mods folder and switch it on in Main Menu > MODS.
Add skin packs (.fgskin) and emote packs (.fgemote) you've saved.

New model

A model of your own (.glb - from Blender, img2threejs, ...) in place of this character. It moves with the character's bones: each part of it bends with the part of the character it's on.
